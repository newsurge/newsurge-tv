      Everything on Retro Surge TV is public domain or clearly licensed, checked title by title: the Fleischer Superman cartoons (1941–43, copyrights never renewed), Prelinger films carrying the Public Domain Dedication, Universal Newsreels (deeded to the U.S. Government), The Big Picture (U.S. Army), NASA footage (courtesy NASA; no endorsement implied), and classic features including Night of the Living Dead (1968), Charade (1963), His Girl Friday (1940) and Carnival of Souls (1962). Played from the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons. Each channel runs on its own clock-set loop — CH 1 ~2 h 58 min · CH 2 the loop, non-stop · CH 3 the local board · CH 4 ~6 h 16 min · CH 5 ~11 h 18 min · CH 6 ~4 h 46 min · CH 7 ~12 h 2 min · CH 8 Retro Weather — always Decatur · CH 9 The Oratory — prayer stream (draft) with a silent-epic film block · CH 10 The Surge Report — with the Morning Edition at 7 AM CT and the Evening Edition at 7 PM CT — so if you leave and come back, the channel has moved on without you, just like the old days. CH 8's soundtrack: "Modern Jazz Samba", "Backed Vibes" and "As I Figure" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY) and "Immersion In Smooth Jazz" by Nick Gordy (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0), via the Internet Archive. CH 9's prayer voices come from the Holy Rosary project; its film block plays silent sacred epics of 1912–1928. CH 2's chessboard is art by KRILLWILL.
