      Every programme on Retro Surge is public domain — the Prelinger Collection, Universal Newsreels, Fleischer Studios' Superman (1941–43), NASA footage (courtesy NASA; no endorsement implied), and classic features including Night of the Living Dead (1968), Charade (1963) and Carnival of Souls (1962). Played straight from the Internet Archive. Each channel runs on its own clock-set loop — CH 1 ~3 h 13 min · CH 2 ~1 h 53 min · CH 3 ~3 h 15 min · CH 4 ~1 h 47 min · CH 5 ~12 h 43 min · CH 6 ~17 h 49 min · CH 7 ~4 h 44 min · CH 8 Retro Weather — always Decatur, around the clock — so if you leave and come back, the channel has moved on without you, just like the old days. CH 8's soundtrack: "Modern Jazz Samba", "Backed Vibes" and "As I Figure" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY) and "Immersion In Smooth Jazz" by Nick Gordy (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0), via the Internet Archive.
